About

How it works

Chapter Check reads the description text you type in the YouTube Studio video-details editor and validates your chapter timestamps against YouTube's published activation rules in real time.

It checks that your first timestamp starts at 0:00, that you have at least 3 chapters, that each chapter is at least 10 seconds long, that timestamps are in strictly ascending order, and that each timecode follows the colon format (M:SS, MM:SS, or H:MM:SS).

Chapter Check is read-only. It never edits the page, never submits the form, and never auto-corrects timestamps. It predicts whether YouTube will turn on chapters; it does not control YouTube.
